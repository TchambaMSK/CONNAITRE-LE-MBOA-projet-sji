Game Rules

  • Choose a difficulty: Easy, Medium, or Hard.
  • Each question has a time limit (30 seconds by default).
  • Answer correctly to earn 1 point.
  • After answering, you'll see immediate feedback and explanation.
  • Complete all questions in a level to unlock the next difficulty.
  • Badges are awarded automatically based on your total score.
  • You can view your score and badges in the ACCOUNTS section.
